# Batch Quality Review
> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT (SOP-QA-150, v1.0). Fictional content for the PharmaGuard AI demo - not a real procedure.

## Purpose and scope
Before release, QA reviews the complete batch record and test results against specifications. This procedure defines the review checklist and the criteria for the status Pass, Under Review or Fail.

## Review checklist
- Confirm all critical quality attributes were tested: dissolution, assay, impurity level and yield.
- Compare each result with its specification. Demo specifications: assay 95% to 105% of label claim, impurity level up to 1.0%, yield 90% to 102%, pH 5.5 to 7.5.
- Confirm process parameters (temperature, humidity, pH, cycle time) stayed within validated ranges.
- Verify that every deviation is documented, assessed and closed.
- Check the equipment cleaning, calibration and maintenance status.

## Status criteria
- Pass: all results within specification and no open deviations.
- Under Review: one or more results near or outside a limit, missing results, or open deviations. Release is on hold until QA closes the review.
- Fail: a critical attribute is out of specification with a confirmed cause, or multiple attributes are out of specification.

## Comparison with historical batches
- Compare the batch with previous batches of the same product, plant and equipment.
- Values that are within specification but statistically unusual should be reviewed for process drift.
- Trending of the same parameter moving in one direction across consecutive batches requires a process review.

## Missing or estimated data
- Missing critical test results must be obtained before release; they must never be estimated.
- A process parameter that was recorded as an estimate (imputed) must be confirmed against the original record.

## Batch disposition
- Release, hold for further testing, reprocess under an approved plan, or reject.
- The final decision is made and signed by an authorised QA person; software output is decision support only.
