# Temperature Excursion
> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT (SOP-QA-305, v1.0). Fictional content for the PharmaGuard AI demo - not a real procedure.

## Purpose and scope
This procedure covers manufacturing-area and process temperature readings that fall outside the validated range. The demo validated range is 18 to 27 degrees Celsius for the processing areas, with relative humidity of 30% to 60%.

## Classification of excursions
- Minor: outside the range by no more than 2 degrees Celsius for less than 1 hour, no product exposure, no adverse trend.
- Major: outside the range by more than 2 degrees Celsius, or longer than 1 hour, or product was open to the environment.
- Critical: extreme readings (for example above 40 degrees Celsius) or excursions with direct evidence of product impact.

## Immediate actions
- Inform the shift supervisor and QA on detection.
- Identify all batches in process in the affected area during the excursion window.
- Verify the reading: check the sensor calibration and compare against a second independent probe.

## Impact assessment
- Determine time above or below limits and the product stage (blending, granulation, drying, coating, filling).
- Review stability data and development data for thermal sensitivity of the active ingredient.
- Check whether dissolution, assay and impurity results for the batch show a shift compared with historical batches.
- A sensor fault with no real excursion must still be documented and the sensor recalibrated.

## Corrective and preventive actions
- Investigate HVAC alarms, door-open events, maintenance activity and utilities failure.
- Trend excursions by plant, area and shift; three or more excursions in a month trigger a CAPA.
- Document the conclusion on batch disposition: release, additional testing, or reject.
